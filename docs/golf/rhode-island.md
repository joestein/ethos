# Rhode Island — Newport National, rank 7, the sole "Public" entry

The selection record for Rhode Island, one of the two roster rows that stood at
`verified: false` after the first programme. It resolves normally on the primary
criterion. Vermont, the other, does not — see `docs/golf/vermont.md`.

## 1. The selection

`https://www.golfdigest.com/courses/guides/rhode-island-best-golf-courses-rankings`,
2025-'26 edition, article dated 29 May 2025. Seven courses, walked from the top
per §2 of `docs/golf/00-ranking.md`:

| Rank | Course | Town | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Newport Country Club | Newport | Private |
| 2 | Wannamoisett Country Club | Rumford | Private |
| 3 | Shelter Harbor Golf Club | Charlestown | Private |
| 4 | The Misquamicut Club | Westerly | Private |
| 5 | Sakonnet Golf Club | Little Compton | Private |
| 6 | Warwick Country Club | Warwick | Private |
| **7** | **Newport National Golf Club: Orchard Course** | **Middletown** | **Public** |

Six `Private` entries walked past, and the seventh is the first and only
`Public` label on the list. Rhode Island is therefore a single-Public state, the
same shape as Iowa (§1 of `docs/golf/iowa.md`), and the selection is forced
rather than chosen: rank 7 is the only entry §2 permits.

This also satisfies §1's open condition 1, which warned that the `Public` label
was confirmed on the every-state summary page but **not** on each of the fifty
per-state pages. It is present on Rhode Island's own per-state page, printed
beside each entry, and that is where it was read here.

## 2. Access rests on the operator, not on the label

The Connecticut row records why this matters: Golf Digest's label for Yale moved
between captures under an identical edition string, so that row rests on the
operator's terms instead. Rhode Island does not have that problem, but it gets
the same treatment.

`https://www.newportnational.com/` states, in the operator's own words:

> "The Golf Course is Open to the Public!"

Address, from the same page: **425 Mitchell's Lane, Middletown, RI 02842**.
Middletown is in **Newport County**.

**No green fee is publishable.** The operator's site carries no rates, no
seasonal variation and no dated pricing at all. That is the same refusal
Massachusetts made for a different reason — there, an unaccompanied-guest rate
existed but carried no dated qualifier — and the rule is the same: an undated
fee is a fee that will be wrong.

## 3. What the operator claims about itself, and how it must be published

All of the following are the operator's own claims on its own site, and each has
to be published as that rather than as fact:

- Architect: *"Legendary golf architect Arthur Hills designed the challenging
  7,244 yard Orchard Course."* The facility credits both courses to
  "Arthur Hills Hills • Forrest • Smith."
- A second, newer **nine-hole Vineyard Course** at the same facility — *"Our
  Newest 9-hole Course."*
- Style: *"The 18-hole links-style course sports sloping fairways, marshes, and
  strategically placed bunkers,"* with views of the Atlantic and Sakonnet
  Passage and *"steady winds coming off the Narragansett Bay."*
- Third-party honours it cites: 8th best links-style course in America (PGA),
  Top 20 New Courses in America (Golfweek), **"#1 Public Access Course in the
  State of Rhode Island" (Golf Digest)**, number one public-access course in New
  England (New England Golf Monthly), and a Luxury Travel Magazine getaways
  listing.

The third of those is worth a note rather than a quiet reprint: the operator
citing Golf Digest as making it the state's #1 *public-access* course is
**consistent with** what §1 above found independently — rank 7 overall, first
`Public` on a mixed list — but it is a different claim from a different Golf
Digest product, and the two must not be blended into one sentence. Publish the
rank from the ranking, and the "#1 public access" line as the operator's own
citation.

**The opening year is absent** from the operator's site and is not published
here. Nothing in this record establishes it.

## 4. What is still missing before a guide can ship

This file records a resolved **selection**. It is not yet a shippable guide, and
the gap is specific:

- **The basecamp is unresearched.** Every other state's guide carries a hotel and
  two or three places to eat, each with a sourced address; §3 of the ranking
  decision treats the basecamp as part of the deliverable, and
  `priv/seed_data/golf/rhode-island.json` cannot be written honestly without it.
- **`second_course` is unsettled.** Connecticut and Massachusetts both name a
  second course at a *different* facility for a second day. Newport National's
  own nine-hole Vineyard Course is at the same address, so it is not the same
  kind of answer, and no second Rhode Island public course has been established
  here.

## 5. Provenance of this file

Unlike the other state records in this directory, this one is **not** backed by a
finder/verifier research pair under `.superpowers/sdd/2026-09-05-golf-courses/`.
Every claim above traces to one of exactly two pages, both named inline and both
read directly: the Golf Digest Rhode Island ranking page and
`newportnational.com`. It contains no claim from recollection and no claim from
a third source. The verifier tally the other state files quote has no counterpart
here, so this record should be treated as a sourced selection awaiting
verification, not as an audited one.
