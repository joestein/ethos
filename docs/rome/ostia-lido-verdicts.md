### Three sources do not say what the finder says.

**1. `ostiaantica.beniculturali.it/.../tor-boacciana/` — the finder's Tor Boacciana straddler evidence
does not exist.** The URL returns HTTP 200, but it serves the **park homepage**. The string
"Boacciana" appears **zero times** in 120,004 bytes. The park's own *Siti e musei* list is: Scavi di
Ostia e Museo Ostiense · Castello di Giulio II e Borgo · Area archeologica dei Porti Imperiali ·
Museo delle Navi · Necropoli di Porto · Complesso della basilica di sant'Ippolito. Tor Boacciana is
not among them. The only statement that the tower is «gestito dal Parco archeologico di Ostia
antica» is a sentence in it.wikipedia citing a dead `comune.roma.it` path. The finder listed this
under "SOURCES ACTUALLY FETCHED" as though it carried the claim. **This materially resolves the
straddler in Ponente's favour** — see §4.

**2. ArchiDiAP does not say 1933–34.** Its header reads, verbatim: `Corso Duca di Genova, 00121 Lido
di Ostia RM, Italia, **-1934**`. It gives a completion year and no start year. The finder's
instruction "publish 1933–34 citing ArchiDiAP" attributes to ArchiDiAP a date it does not carry.
ArchiDiAP also describes the fountain figures as «due **muse**», not as double-tailed sirens.

**3. The Biblioteche di Roma page carries none of the figures cited to it.** No 31,000 books, no
3,100 DVDs, no 2,500 CDs, no Toscanelli address. Those numbers are it.wikipedia's, taken from
Biblioteche di Roma's OpenData export, not from the library page. What the page *does* carry is
better than anything the finder had: the current substitute address, a phone number, and a full
2026 hours table. See §3.

## 5. TIER RULING — **GUIDE**, on 14 surviving places

The finder recommended TOWN-PAGE and argued it from character: no square, no promenade set-piece, no
beach institution, no station. That is an honest description of the material and the wrong test. The
test is what the corpus has actually shipped, and I checked it mechanically rather than reasoning
about it:

```
guide  places=  9  sallustiano.json
guide  places= 10  ludovisi.json
guide  places= 18  san-saba.json
…
guide  places= 88  campo-marzio.json
```

**Every one of the 21 shipped Rome files is tier `guide`, including Sallustiano at nine places and
Ludovisi at ten.** Fourteen survivors sit comfortably above the shipped floor and above San Saba.

The survivors, and they are not thin:

1. Tor San Michele · 2. Idroscalo di Ostia · 3. Porto Turistico di Roma · 4. Centro Habitat
Mediterraneo · 5. Parco Letterario Pier Paolo Pasolini · 6. Ex Colonia marina Vittorio Emanuele III ·
7. Teatro del Lido di Ostia · 8. Biblioteca Elsa Morante · 9. Ufficio postale di Ostia Lido with the
Fontana delle Sirene · 10. Cineland in the ex Meccanica Romana · 11. Palazzo dell'ex GIL · 12. Scuola
Fratelli Garrone · 13. Chiesa di Santa Monica · 14. Parco Willy Ferrero.
**15. Tor Boacciana**, on the straddler ruling above.

Three carry first-party published hours — LIPU, the Biblioteca Elsa Morante (a full 2026 table), and
the Porto Turistico's control tower and office. Four are addressed 1930s buildings with named
architects. That is a guide.

**What the guide must say about itself,** and the finder is right that it is the whole framing: this
page is the western third of Ostia and does not hold the seafront. Two sourced facts carry that
honestly, and the finder missed both. The Ponente article states that the quartiere was created by
**delibera 2453 of 13 September 1961**, by the Commissario Straordinario Francesco Diana, by
splitting the old "Lido di Ostia" quartiere — officially instituted in 1940, in existence since May
1933 — and that Q. XXXIII is **«il terzultimo quartiere di Roma»**, third from last in the official
series. A position in an official series is expressly not a superlative, and it is a far better
opening than "Rome's beach", which this row may not claim.

## 6. WHAT THE FINDER GOT WRONG

**It got the hard thing right.** The headline finding is correct, well evidenced and the most
valuable thing in the file; the exclusion list is accurate against both stations' articles and the
Castel Fusano article; the Colonia `vincolo` sentence, the Cineland and library superlatives, the
"first teatro di cintura", the heron colony, the port's "25 Years Experience" and its ten-minute
drive were all caught correctly. The 404 was reported honestly and kept out of reader text.

Against that:

1. **It cited an institutional page that does not carry the claim.** The Tor Boacciana page at
   ostiaantica.beniculturali.it 200s but is the park homepage; the word "Boacciana" is not on it.
   The finder listed it under "sources actually fetched" and rested half a straddler on it.
2. **It attributed a date to ArchiDiAP that ArchiDiAP does not give.** "-1934", not 1933–34.
3. **It cited the wrong page for the library's holdings** and, in doing so, missed the substitute
   address, the phone number, the full 2026 hours table and the Fondo Ostia — all first party.
4. **It reproduced three banned priority claims in its own prose**: "Ostia's first factory", the
   Idroscalo's "first air link between Italy and the North African coasts", and it quoted the post
   office's Murano-glass sentence from an article whose next clause is «novità assoluta».
5. **It missed five more hazards** in sources it recommends: the Tor San Michele PDF's «una delle più
   importanti vedette costiere», the post office's «quasi 80 anni» and «unico nel suo genere», the
   library page's «una delle più importanti letterate», and LIPU's «a soli 10 minuti».
6. **It invented two conflicts that are not conflicts.** Tor San Michele's 18 m and 24.1 m are the
   tower and the tower-plus-turret, stated as such in the same sentence of it.wikipedia; Cineland's
   15,000 m² and 14,000 m² are covered area and walkable floor area. Both pairs publish.
7. **It missed three real conflicts**: Tor San Michele finished 1568 (Roma Capitale) or 1569
   (it.wikipedia, citing Colonna 2013); the Meccanica Romana built at Pio Perrone's will
   (it.wikipedia) or Mussolini's (Roma Capitale); the post-office fountain figures as sirens
   (it.wikipedia) or muses (ArchiDiAP, the source it.wikipedia cites).
8. **It mislocated a banner.** The Teatro del Lido's `{{F}}` covers the whole article, not a section,
   so its entire 1997–2010 history is unsourced Wikipedia and cannot be published bare.
9. **It missed the zone's own founding facts** — delibera 2453 of 13 September 1961, the 1940 and
   May 1933 antecedents, and *terzultimo quartiere* — which are precisely the sourced, non-superlative
   material the page needs to open with.
10. **It missed the best hook in the zone**: John Paul II at Santa Monica on 8 May 1983 and Francis
    there on 3 June 2018, both on vatican.va.
11. **It under-ruled the tier.** Nine-place and ten-place Rome files ship as guides. Fourteen is a
