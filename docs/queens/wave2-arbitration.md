# Queens Wave 2 — ARBITRATION RULINGS

**Scope:** Jackson Heights, Sunnyside, Woodside. Written before the three authors begin, so each has
an allow-list rather than a judgement call.

**Evidence ranking used throughout (highest first):**
1. The place's own street address, including the neighborhood name printed in it.
2. What the best-evidenced source says explicitly (LPC designation reports, NYC Parks agency pages,
   NPS/NRHP records, DCP GeoSearch/PAD, the institution's own site).
3. Community-district and NTA/boundary definitions the verdicts confirm.

**Standing rule:** a place assigned to another neighborhood may be MENTIONED in prose, if a confirmed
verdict supports the mention, only as the neighbouring place it is — never as this page's own — and
it gets no place record in this file.

## 2. THE TWO SUBJECTS MOST LIKELY TO BE CLAIMED TWICE

### 2.1 Jackson Heights Historic District — **JACKSON HEIGHTS**, uncontested

LP-1831's own metes-and-bounds run 78th Street/34th Avenue east to 88th Street and south to
Roosevelt Avenue — every street named lies between 76th and 88th. Sunnyside Gardens appears nowhere
in LP-1831's boundary and neither sibling dossier claims the district. Neither Sunnyside nor Woodside
may publish it; neither has a source that would let them.

### 2.2 Sunnyside Gardens Historic District — **SUNNYSIDE**, uncontested

LPC LP-2258 plus NPS NRIS 84002919 (listed 9/7/1984), *"Roughly bounded by Queens Blvd., 43rd and
52nd Sts. Barnett and Skillman Aves."* Woodside §1.8 states plainly that Woodside makes no claim.
Eastern edge is **52nd Street**, per LPC's curbline language and NPS independently — Woodside must
not treat 51st or 52nd Street frontage inside that line as its own.

Two riders for the Sunnyside author, from its own verdicts: publish the district's size as **~54
acres or not at all** (77 acres is refuted; 76.67 acres is the 1924 purchase, a different quantity),
and the courts are **twelve, plus two Phipps complexes** — that count is LPC's own, so it may ship.

### 2.3 Sunnyside Gardens Park — **SUNNYSIDE**, decisively

48-21 39th Avenue, DCP locality Sunnyside, ZIP 11104, inside LPC's designated district. The park's
own banner naming Sunnyside **and** Woodside is a service-area statement, not a location.
**Woodside gets no record and must not read the banner as a claim.**
