# Queens Wave 3 — ARBITRATION

**Neighborhoods:** Corona and Elmhurst (Community District 4); Forest Hills and Rego Park
(Community District 6). Four authors write in parallel and cannot see each other's files. This
document is binding on all four.

**Inputs read in full:** `corona-verdicts.md`, `elmhurst-verdicts.md`, `forest-hills-verdicts.md`,
`rego-park-verdicts.md`.

**Evidence rank used for every ruling below:**
1. The place's own street address and the neighborhood name printed in it.
2. What the best-evidenced source says explicitly (LPC designation reports, NYC Parks agency pages,
   NPS/NRHP records, DCP GeoSearch/PLUTO/DOB, the institution's own site).
3. Community-district, NTA and boundary definitions the verdicts confirm.

**Standing rule.** A place assigned to another neighborhood may be MENTIONED in prose where a
confirmed verdict supports the mention, only as the neighbouring place it is — never as this page's
own — and it gets **no place record** in this file.

**Second standing rule.** Nothing in this document authorises a claim. A ruling says *who may*
publish a place if a CONFIRMED verdict supports it. Where the winning file has no confirmed fact for
a place, the place ships nowhere.

### 3.2 FOREST PARK → **FOREST HILLS**, weakly, and with three hard constraints

This one was close, and two verifiers disagreed with each other. I am ruling, and I say what would
overturn it.

**For Forest Hills:** it is the only neighborhood in the corpus that ships a page and is (a)
contiguous with the park — the Forest Hills verifier measured the minimum distance between the
Forest Park and Forest Hills NTA rings at **3.3 metres**, against **567.9 metres** to Rego Park;
(b) **named for it in two independent primary sources** — LPC LP-2066: *"Changing the name to Forest
Hills because of its high ground and its **proximity** to the beautiful terrain of Forest Park"*, and
NYC Parks' Yellowstone sign: *"Its name is derived from its **proximity** to Forest Park"*; and (c)
among the ZIPs (`11375`) and community boards (`6`) on the park's own NYC Parks record. The Corona
and Elmhurst verifiers both independently ruled it Forest Hills' and not theirs.

**Against:** the Rego Park verifier sampled the park's own polygon and got `QN8291 Forest Park` at
the centre, `QN0901 Kew Gardens`, `QN0902 Richmond Hill` and `QN0503 Glendale` at the edges, and
**no sample landed in `QN0602 Forest Hills`**; its street address geocodes `80-30 PARK LANE, Forest
Park, NY, USA`, ZIP `11415` (Kew Gardens), `cd 482`; and the park's own reader poll names Richmond
Hill, Kew Gardens, Forest Hills, Glendale and Woodhaven as candidate home neighborhoods. Its
position was that the park belongs to no file in this wave.

**RULING: Forest Hills, and it was close.** The tiebreaker is that no competing claimant ships a
page, and a marquee place that appears nowhere is as much a corpus failure as one that appears
twice. **What would overturn it:** a Woodhaven, Richmond Hill, Glendale or Kew Gardens page entering
the corpus, or a dedicated Forest Park page. If either happens, this ruling is void and Forest Hills
keeps only the etymology.

**Three constraints, binding on the Forest Hills author:**

1. **Do not publish "80-30 Park Lane, Forest Hills, NY 11375."** That address is `Forest Park, NY
   11415` in DCP's own geocoder. Print `80-30 Park Lane, Queens, NY 11415`, or print no address and
   give the Parks location string `Myrtle Ave, Union Tpke, Park Lane S`.
2. **Do not write that Forest Park is in Forest Hills.** Both primary sources say *proximity*. Write
   that Forest Hills is named for the park and borders it. Nothing more.
3. **Do not annex its interior features.** Oak Ridge, the Overlook, the Forest Park Carousel, the
   George Seuffert, Sr. Bandshell, Victory Field and the Richmond Hill War Memorial are
   Woodhaven/Richmond Hill-side features. Link; do not annex. The Nature Center's "currently closed"
   line is undated — do not publish it in the present tense.

**Corona, Elmhurst and Rego Park may not create Forest Park.** Corona is ZIP 11368 and CB 3/4,
neither of which appears in the park's record, and does not touch it. Elmhurst's only Wikipedia
mention of Forest Park describes the 17th-century *Town of Newtown*, not the modern neighborhood, and
must not be used to claim it. Rego Park may state the Parks walkability line on the Remsen Family
Cemetery page (*"Forest Park (0.51 miles)"*) — that is a distance, not a claim.

## 6. RULES THAT BIND ALL FOUR FILES

1. **The eight `CitiFieldPlaces` holdings are off-limits to all four**, as records and as prose.
   Link to `/g/citi-field-guide`.
2. **A DOHMH/DCWP inspection record establishes existence and address and NOTHING else.** No
   adjective, no dish, no founding date, no "beloved", no cuisine narrative — not even the category
   noun in prose. Names are not evidence of neighborhood, either: "Rego Park Best Pizza" is outside
   the Rego Park NTA.
3. **Never publish a count from a live dataset as a fact.** The tallies drifted between finder and
   verifier in three of the four files. Name the items; do not total them.
4. **No research-process prose.** Never write about what the page, the series or the research did or
   did not find. The reader is not the researcher.
5. **No unrestated modifiers on confirmed facts, and no unsourced superlatives.**
6. **Banned phrasings, enforced on every string including captions and link notes:** "steps from",
   "next door", "across the street from", "a few doors down", "around the corner", "down/up the
   street", "down/up the block", "within walking distance", "N blocks north/south/east/west",
   "north/south/east/west of the station|stadium|ballpark|arena|venue|site". The literal senses trip
   too — write "stairs", and "along the block".
7. **Where sources genuinely conflict, give the range or attribute. Never pick one and state it
   flatly**, and never average.
