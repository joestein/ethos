# Wave 1 research artifact — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens

Written in fix round 1 of Task 7, because the wave shipped without the Step 2 artifact the
brief required. Task 1's `rooms.md` is the room census (names, addresses, genre). **This
file is the evidence for every concrete claim the prose makes beyond that** — seat counts,
equipment, chef names, founding years, awards, architecture, hours, music, closures.

Method: each claim was researched from scratch against operator sites and 2024-2026
coverage. **Nothing here was copied out of the already-published prose.** Where a claim
could not be established, the verdict is DELETE and the sentence was cut from the JSON;
where it was established, the verdict is VERIFIED and the sentence stands (sometimes
rewritten to say only what the source says).

Verdict key: **VERIFIED** — source establishes it, prose keeps it.
**DELETE** — no source found, sentence removed from the JSON.
**CORRECTED** — source contradicts the published wording; prose rewritten to the source.

## Tally

251 claims researched across 63 rooms and three guides.

**Verified and recorded: 210** — 184 VERIFIED as the prose already had them, 26 CORRECTED,
where the sentence stayed but was rewritten to say only what the source says.
**Deleted: 40.** Plus one place, Boucherie Union Square, removed outright.

The pattern in the deletions is consistent and worth carrying into wave 2: the claims that
failed were **numbers nobody publishes** (seat counts, distances in blocks or miles, wine-list
sizes, table counts), **decor adjectives** (marble, travertine, brass, glazed brick), **founding
years without a source**, and **comparatives** ("the older of the two", "the most formal of
them"). Addresses, genre, menu structure and operator history almost always check out.
Write those; leave the rest alone.
