## Block C — Bartow-Pell Mansion Museum

100. **Discrepancy 4 — the two names. Verdict: not a conflict. The institution is
     the "Bartow-Pell Mansion Museum"; the building is the "Bartow–Pell
     Mansion".** The museum's own homepage writes "Bartow-Pell Mansion Museum"
     and its history timeline records, for 1959, "The name Pell is added to
     acknowledge the importance of the Pell family." A body's own site is
     authoritative for its own identity, which is exactly the exception rule 4
     allows. The LPC boroughwide list independently carries the interior
     designation as "Bartow-Pell Mansion Museum Interior", corroborating the
     institutional name from a third party. **Ship the place as `Bartow-Pell
     Mansion Museum`, slug `bartow-pell-mansion-museum`, hyphen not en dash.**
     One correction to the finder: the 1959 change added "Pell" to a name that
     was then "Bartow Mansion Museum and Gardens" — it did not adopt the full
     modern name in one step, and the source does not say the whole name changed
     that year.
101. Address 895 Shore Road, Pelham Bay Park, Bronx, NY 10464 — **confirmed** by
     both Wikipedia and the museum's own site (identity and location, permitted).
102. Phone (718) 885-1461 — **confirmed**; the site writes it "718. 885.1461".
103. Robert Bartow acquired 30 acres in 1836; mansion constructed 1836–1842 —
     **confirmed** by both sources; the museum adds he "moved in with his wife
     and children in 1842".
104. Gray-stone Greek Revival mansion with Federal interiors — **confirmed**:
     "a Greek Revival facade and federal interiors".
105. Built by Robert Bartow and his wife Maria Lorillard Bartow — **uncertain**.
     Re-fetches attribute construction to Robert Bartow; the wife's name did not

106. Third residence on the site, Pell houses circa 1675 and circa 1790 —

107. Bartow and Pell families alternately owned through 1888, when NYC acquired
     it for Pelham Bay Park — **confirmed**.
108. International Garden Club leased from 1914 — **confirmed** by both.
109. Led by Mrs. Charles Frederick Hoffman 1914–1921 — **uncertain**, not
     reproduced in re-fetches. Do not ship.
110. Delano & Aldrich restoration dates — **refuted as attributed.** The finder

113. **Discrepancy 6 — National Historic Landmark year, 1976 vs 1977. Verdict:

114. NRHP listing as "Bartow-Pell Mansion and Carriage House", "Pelham Bay Park,
