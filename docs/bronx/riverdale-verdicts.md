## C. Boundary findings — adjudicated

### Finding 1 — Henry Hudson Bridge and Monument → Spuyten Duyvil

**Ratified as the finder concluded.** Re-fetched both articles. The bridge's
own lead: "It connects Spuyten Duyvil in the Bronx with Inwood in Manhattan...
via the Henry Hudson Parkway"; infobox locale: "Spuyten Duyvil, Bronx and
Inwood, Manhattan." The monument sits in Henry Hudson Memorial Park, within
Spuyten Duyvil, dedicated January 6, 1938, per Spuyten Duyvil's own article,
which separately concedes "some consider it to be the southernmost part of
Riverdale" — a loose framing, not a placement. **Neither ships as a Riverdale
place.**

### Finding 2 — Fieldston Historic District, Christ Church, Riverdale Presbyterian Church

Three separate items with three separate rulings, not one.

**Fieldston Historic District: excluded from this guide.** The boroughwide
landmarks list categorizes this specific designation under location
"Fieldston" — a distinct label, not nested under "Riverdale" the way it nests
Robert Colgate House under "Hudson Hill, Bronx" (a Riverdale subsection). That
distinct administrative label matches the roster's own decision to carry
`fieldston` as a separate row. Publishing the historic district here risks
exactly the double-claim the dispatch warns against if a future Fieldston
wave ships its own guide. **Ruling: belongs to the `fieldston` roster row,
not written up as a Riverdale place.** (Its facts are independently confirmed
— Jan 10, 2006 designation, 252 houses, both dates matching on Fieldston's own
article and the boroughwide list — the exclusion is a boundary call, not a
fact refutation.)

**Christ Church and Riverdale Presbyterian Church: resolved as Riverdale, not
Fieldston — and both had a dedicated Wikipedia article the finder's guessed
titles missed.** `Christ_Church_(Riverdale,_Bronx)` and
`Riverdale_Presbyterian_Church` both 404, as the finder found; the correct
titles are `Christ_Church_(Bronx,_New_York)` and
`Riverdale_Presbyterian_Church_Complex`. Both dedicated articles state
"Riverdale" as the neighborhood — "Christ Church Riverdale is a historic
Episcopal parish church... in Riverdale, Bronx" and "Riverdale Presbyterian
Church is a historic Presbyterian church... in the Riverdale neighborhood of
the Bronx" — agreeing with the boroughwide list's "Riverdale" categorization
for both. Fieldston's own article listing them under its "Outside the
historic district" heading is consistent with this: per a direct check of
that section, the heading means still-within-Fieldston-but-outside-the-2006-
district-boundary for the buildings that stay there (e.g. Hadley House), but
for these two specific churches the buildings' own dedicated, more specific
articles override the general neighborhood page. **Ruling: both ship as
Riverdale places.** See the place blocks below for the address corrections
this surfaced.

### Finding 3 — Fonthill Castle / University of Mount Saint Vincent → Riverdale

**Ruled for Riverdale**, on a corrected reading of the sourcing balance, which
was already 3-1 in Riverdale's favor and is not weakened by re-fetching.
Correction to the finder's framing: North Riverdale's own article does not
carry a heading of "landmarks in North Riverdale proper" — the college is
named once, in a "Notable places" list ("the neighborhood is home to the
College of Mount Saint Vincent"), and once more in the lead ("The northernmost
point in New York City is at the Hudson River on the campus of the College of
Mount Saint Vincent"). Both are genuine claims, but neither is the specific
per-landmark heading the finder described. Against that: Riverdale's own
article, the university's own article ("is situated in Riverdale, Bronx"),
and the boroughwide landmarks list (categorized "Riverdale") all agree, and —
unlike Fieldston above — **no source anywhere in this file uses "North
Riverdale" as a distinct administrative label**; it is only ever a
neighborhood-description subsection, not a landmarks-list category. That
absence of a parallel administrative split is what tips this the other way
from Fieldston. **Ships as a Riverdale place**, current name **University of
Mount Saint Vincent** (renamed from College of Mount Saint Vincent in 2024,
confirmed on the university's own article), with Fonthill Castle folded in as
its landmark building per the sub-attraction rule.

### Finding 4 (new — not raised by the finder) — Gaelic Park → Kingsbridge

The finder listed Gaelic Park as "not pursued... flagged for a later pass, not
excluded on any negative finding," treating its omission as a research-budget
gap rather than a boundary problem. **It is a boundary problem, and the
correct exclusion is Kingsbridge, not Riverdale.** Gaelic Park's own dedicated
Wikipedia article states plainly: "located at West 240th Street and Broadway
in **the Kingsbridge neighborhood of the Bronx**." Riverdale's own article
mentions Gaelic Park in passing without asserting it is in Riverdale. Applying
the same rule used for the Henry Hudson Bridge (Finding 1) and the two
churches above — the specific, dedicated source for the place itself outranks
a general neighborhood article's passing mention — **Gaelic Park belongs to
Kingsbridge, a separate in-scope wave-3 roster row, and does not ship here.**
Flag for whichever wave researches Kingsbridge.
