## The three adjudications

**1. The Belmont Stakes' final year at Morris Park Racecourse — 1904 or 1905?**
**A genuine, unresolved conflict — drop the specific end year.** The main
neighborhood article says the Belmont "continued to be run there until 1905";
the dedicated Morris Park Racecourse article says it hosted "the Belmont
Stakes from 1890 through 1904" and separately gives the track's own final
race as October 15, 1904. This is not a "1900 vs. 1901" case that dissolves
on a closer read — both articles independently and specifically commit to a
number, and the numbers disagree by one year, with no way from either source
alone to say which is transcription drift and which is fact. Per the
authoring contract, an uncertain history-fact specific may be kept only by
removing it: publish "the racecourse hosted the Belmont Stakes in the early
1890s through the mid-1900s" only if a vaguer form is wanted, or simply state
it hosted the Belmont without naming the final year. Do not hedge with
"reportedly" or "around."

**2. The racecourse's "1904" (dedicated article) vs. "1910" (main article) —
does this dissolve?** **Yes, on inspection, and it should be reported as
resolved rather than left open.** These are not two claims about the same
fact: the dedicated article's "1889 to 1904" describes when **thoroughbred
racing itself** operated; the main article's "1889 until 1910" describes how
long **the racecourse property existed** before the fire ended its post-racing
life (automobile racing, the 1907–1909 Aeronautic Society lease, the 1910
