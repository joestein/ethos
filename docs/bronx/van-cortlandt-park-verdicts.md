# Van Cortlandt Park — verdicts

Role: INDEPENDENT VERIFIER. The finder's reasoning was not consulted beyond the
artifact itself. Every line below is a verdict: `confirmed`, `refuted`, or
`uncertain`. Where a claim is refuted or corrected, the source's actual wording
and the re-fetched URL are given.

Input: `docs/bronx/van-cortlandt-park-finder.md`.

Method: WebSearch attempted once ("Van Cortlandt House Museum history Bronx
landmark designation") — confirmed exhausted, 200/200, as the finder reported.
Everything below is WebFetch re-fetch of every URL the finder cited that
carries a number, date, name or superlative, plus several boundary/orientation
pages re-fetched in full for the finder's boundary-check section.
`nycgovparks.org` and `web.archive.org` were not attempted, per the dispatch.

**Headline: this is not a clean pass, and it is not a thin one either.** One
genuine cross-source size/rank discrepancy the finder never flagged (park
acreage and rank, block A), one NRHP date the source itself states
inconsistently between its infobox and body text (block B), two casualty-count
and quotation corrections carried through, and — most importantly for the tier
call — **four candidate features the finder researched, sourced, and then
declined to promote survive on the identical evidentiary standard the finder
applied to the ones it did write up.** One new fact (the Vault Hill
records-hiding history) turned up on re-fetch of a source the finder had
already cited and marked "nothing found."

**Ruling on C6 (Mosholu Golf Course boundary):** The course's own dedicated
article places it explicitly *inside* Van Cortlandt Park ("at Van Cortlandt
Park") — the neighborhood clause describes the surrounding area, not a
competing container. The course is operated by NYC Parks as a unit of Van
Cortlandt Park, sits adjacent to the park's own golf course (Van Cortlandt
Golf Course) and shares an opening-year sentence with it in the park's own
article ("owing to the Van Cortlandt Golf Course's immense popularity, the
Mosholu Links also opened that year"). **Ships under this row.** If a future
`woodlawn` guide is built, it should reference Mosholu Golf Course as a
bordering relationship, never claim it as a `woodlawn` place — the same
non-overlap principle City Island's verifier applied to the City Island
Bridge.
